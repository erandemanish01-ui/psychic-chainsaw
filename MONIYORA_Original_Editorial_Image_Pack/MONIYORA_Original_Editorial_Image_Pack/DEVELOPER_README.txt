Do not slice them into product images, use embedded mock text as live website text, or represent depicted branded mock products as real inventory. The developer should create the actual website sections using separately sourced original/licensed photographs and real product photography.

RIGHTS / CLEARANCE
These are newly generated visual concepts and were not sourced from OXO London. They are not a guarantee of trademark clearance, exclusivity, or freedom from every possible third-party rights issue. Before commercial launch, review generated imagery for accidental marks/logos, retain the original generation records, and obtain legal review where needed. For real products, use supplier-authorised images or commissioned product photography with written commercial usage rights.
